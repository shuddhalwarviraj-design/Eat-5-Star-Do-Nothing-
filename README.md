🌌 Explore the galaxy. 🚀 Fly your spacecraft. 🪐 Discover the wonders of space!

## 🌟 About The Project

**GALAXY MISSIONS** is an interactive, browser-based 3D space exploration game developed for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 – Space Mission Design Game**.

The project aims to make space exploration more engaging and accessible through interactive gameplay, 3D environments, spacecraft selection, and space-themed challenges.

Players can choose their spacecraft, select missions, adjust difficulty levels, and explore different space scenarios while learning through an entertaining gaming experience.

The goal is to combine technology, creativity, and space exploration to inspire curiosity about the universe.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Make space exploration interactive and enjoyable.
- Introduce players to different space-related challenges.
- Encourage curiosity about planets, asteroids, and the universe.
- Provide an engaging 3D gaming experience.
- Combine educational concepts with entertainment.

## 🎮 Game Features

### 🪐 1. Four Exciting Space Missions

The game features four different mission modes:

| Mission | Description |
|---|---|
| ☄️ Asteroid Dodge | Survive for 60 seconds in an asteroid storm. |
| 🌕 Moon Landing | Attempt a soft landing on the designated landing pad. |
| 🌌 Galaxy Explorer | Explore the solar system and discover interesting space facts. |
| 💎 Space Collector | Collect space crystals while avoiding dangerous mines. |

### 🚀 2. Choose Your Spacecraft

Players can select from three different spacecraft:

- **FALCON** – Balanced spacecraft.
- **NOVA** – Faster spacecraft.
- **TITAN** – Stronger spacecraft with increased durability.

Each spacecraft has different speed and health characteristics.

### 🎚️ 3. Difficulty Levels

Choose your preferred challenge:

- 🟢 Easy
- 🟡 Medium
- 🔴 Hard

### 🏆 4. Achievements & Trophies

Players can unlock achievements by completing different activities and challenges.

Achievements include:
- First Flight
- Asteroid Survivor
- Soft Landing
- Cosmic Explorer
- Crystal Hunter
- Hard Mode Hero
- Alien Contact

### 🎵 5. Space Audio Experience

Enjoy an immersive space-themed audio experience with:

- Deep Nebula
- Cosmic Drift
- Starlight Synth
- Volume adjustment
- Sound on/off controls

### 📱 6. Mobile-Friendly Controls

The game includes touch-based controls for mobile devices, making space exploration accessible on different screen sizes.

### 💾 7. Progress Saving

The game uses browser LocalStorage to save player settings and game progress.


## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open the game in your browser.
2. Enter your pilot name.
3. Click **START EXPLORING**.
4. Select your preferred mission.
5. Choose a difficulty level.
6. Select your spacecraft.
7. Start your space adventure!
8. Complete missions and unlock achievements.

### Controls

- Use the arrow keys for spacecraft movement.
- Use the Space key for thrust.
- On mobile devices, use the on-screen directional buttons and thrust control.
- Use the in-game buttons to pause, restart, or exit a mission.
