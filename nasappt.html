FLIGHT OPERATIONS // 02

FOUR CORE SPACE MISSIONS

Every mission tests a distinct piloting discipline—combining evasive reflexes, Newtonian thrust physics, planetary navigation, and risk-reward resource collection.

1. ASTEROID DODGE

Survive a relentless 60-second rock storm in deep space as asteroid velocity and spawn density escalate over time.

  • Objective: Survive full 60.0s timer
  • Scoring: +10 pts/sec + 3 pts per dodged rock
  • Defense: Hull shields + 1.5s post-hit invulnerability
2. MOON LANDING

Control descent angle, rotational attitude, and main engine burn to touch down softly on the illuminated green landing pad.

  • Gravity: Constant -2.2 m/s² lunar pull
  • Touchdown: Upright tilt (< 0.35 rad) & safe speed
  • Scoring: Fuel remaining × 8 + soft-landing bonus
3. GALAXY EXPLORER

Free-roam 3D flight across an orbiting solar system. Approach all 8 planets to unlock real NASA-style planetary facts.

  • Sub-Missions: Inner Planets, Gas Giants, Ice Giants
  • Scoring: +100 pts/planet + 300 pts/sector bonus
  • Propulsion: Variable thrust + Shift afterburner boost
4. SPACE COLLECTOR

Harvest glowing cyan energy crystals while weaving past volatile red proximity mines in a 45-second high-speed run.

  • Goal: Score 100+ points within 45.0s
  • Crystals: +10 points per cyan octahedron
  • Hazards: Red dodecahedron mines strip hull shield
